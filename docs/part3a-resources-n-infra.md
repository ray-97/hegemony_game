## **Part III: Infrastructure, Factions, and Asymmetric Warfare**

### **6. The Resource & Infrastructure Engine**
Capital (`$CAP`) is the game's sole liquid resource. Instead of managing complex physical inventory (wood, steel), State Actors spend `$CAP` to build infrastructure that levels up specific **Strategic Sectors**. A region's Dominance ($D_r$) and Yield are derived from the balance of these sectors.

#### **6.1 The Three Strategic Sectors**
* **Sector 1: Energy & Power Grid**
    * *Effect:* Determines the **Base Yield** of `$CAP` per turn distributed to `$BOND` holders.
* **Sector 2: Technology & Manufacturing**
    * *Effect:* Determines the **Build Speed / Cost Reduction**. High tech allows the region to build further infrastructure at a steep `$CAP` discount and reduces defensive $DI$ costs.
* **Sector 3: Logistics & Trade Routes**
    * *Effect:* Determines **Liquidity Flow ($DI$ Generation)**. Generates passive Diplomatic Influence and reduces the AMM "Trading Tax" for foreign Traders.

### **7. Infrastructure Asymmetry (Risk vs. Reward)**
Infrastructure upgrades are non-linear. State Actors must choose between **Type A (Wide & Fragile)** or **Type B (Deep & Fortified)** builds. All costs are paid in liquid `$CAP`.

#### **Sector 1: Energy (Yield Engine)**
* **Type A: Fossil / LNG Infrastructure**
    * *Profile:* Low Cost, Instant Build. High immediate yield.
    * *Vulnerability:* **Supply Chain Shock** (Cheap for rivals to target).
* **Type B: Nuclear Power Grid**
    * *Profile:* Extreme Cost, 3-Turn Build. Massive, permanent yield that resists minor shocks.
    * *Vulnerability:* **Cyber-Meltdown** (Catastrophic fallout if successfully attacked).

#### **Sector 2: Technology (Scaling Engine)**
* **Type A: Rare Earth / Heavy Processing**
    * *Profile:* Medium Cost. Reduces the `$CAP` cost of future regional infrastructure by 25%.
    * *Vulnerability:* **Proxy Insurgency / Labor Strike** (Disrupts supply chain, halting construction).
* **Type B: Advanced Semiconductor Fabs**
    * *Profile:* High Cost. Cyber supremacy. Halves the $DI required to maintain the region's "Veto Shield."
    * *Vulnerability:* **Corporate Espionage** (Steals tech advantages rather than destroying the facility).

#### **Sector 3: Logistics (Liquidity Engine)**
* **Type A: Deepwater Ports**
    * *Profile:* High Cost. Generates massive passive $DI. Reduces AMM Foreign Trading Tax to 0%.
    * *Vulnerability:* **Naval Blockade** (Freezes capital movement).
* **Type B: Trans-Continental Rail Network**
    * *Profile:* Medium Cost (Requires adjacency). Initiates a "Vampire Attack" that steals a percentage of the connected region's yield.
    * *Vulnerability:* **Border Skirmish** (Severs the link and traps moving `$CAP`).

### **8. The 5 Hegemonic Regions**
The board is divided into five asymmetric regions, each with distinct starting advantages and systemic vulnerabilities.

| Region ID | Thematic Focus | Competitive Advantage | Systemic Vulnerability | Playstyle |

| **North American Federation**| Financial Hegemon | Starts with the highest baseline `$CAP` reserves and AMM liquidity. | **Political Friction:** Covert Ops launched *by* this region cost 2x $DI. | Capital-heavy defense; relies on sanctions over kinetic action. |
| **Eurozone Bloc** | Institutional Power| Generates 2x passive $DI$ per turn. Unmatched defensive "Veto Shields." | **Sluggish Bureaucracy:** Infrastructure takes an extra turn to build/activate. | Slow, stable yield-farming haven for safe `$BOND`s. |
| **Gulf-MENA Kingdom** | The Energy Cartel | Starts with Max Energy grids. Prints massive baseline `$CAP` yield. | **Fragile Logistics:** Minimal defense against naval blockades. Low natural $DI$. | High-yield cash cow; must aggressively bribe/sanction to survive. |
| **Global South Coalition** | Emerging Frontier | Lowest baseline $D_r$, meaning their AMM markets have extreme volatility. | **Structural Instability:** Highest base probability of falling to `Contested` status. | High-risk, high-reward; the ultimate proxy war playground. |

### **9. The Expanded Covert Operations Menu**
State Actors cannot custom-design attacks. They must fund specific operations from a menu designed to trigger the **Geopolitical Trilemma** (forcing a region to sacrifice Yield, Security, or Liquidity). 

*Operations require upfront `$CAP` (which becomes AMM liquidity) and burn `$DI`.*

| Operation Name | Target Infrastructure | The Ripple Effect (Market Impact) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. The Supply Shock** | Fossil / LNG | **Yield Freeze:** Target Energy yield slashed by 80% for 2 turns. Traders panic-sell `$BOND`s, tanking the region's $MCI. |
| **2. Cyber-Meltdown** | Nuclear Grid | **Systemic Contagion:** Facility destroyed. Fallout causes a 15% Dominance ($D_r$) drop in all *adjacent* regions. |
| **3. Corporate Espionage**| Semiconductor Fabs | **Soft-Power Siphon:** Attacker steals 50% of the target's held $DI balance, leaving the target defenseless for follow-up attacks. |
| **4. Proxy Insurgency** | Rare Earth / Mfg. | **Capital Sink:** Facility is contested. Target must pay the original `$CAP` build cost *again* to repair it, or it degrades to rubble. |
| **5. Naval Blockade** | Deepwater Ports | **Capital Controls:** Freezes the AMM. For 1 turn, Traders cannot unstake `$BOND`s, and no new "YES" shares can be bought. |
| **6. Border Skirmish** | Rail Networks | **Trade Severance:** Terminates "Vampire Attack" yield theft. Applies a massive Volatility Penalty ($V$) to both regions' Dominance scores. |
